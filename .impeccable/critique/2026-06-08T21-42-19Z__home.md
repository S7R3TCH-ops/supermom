### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment: Not AI-generated.** Genuine point of view — dark hero briefing, DEEP_ROSE urgency vocabulary, flying-icon GO button. Semantic color vocabulary is consistent and designed. No side-stripe borders, no hero-metric tiles, no identical card grids. What exists is implementation drift: inline font-weight overrides beyond what's loaded, a section header bypassing its component, contrast values that crack in light mode.

**Deterministic scan:** One finding — `transition: height` at Home.jsx:1118 (layout-transition). Confirmed real.

### Priority Issues

**[P1] Font weights 800/900 (Inter) and 700 (Fraunces) are synthesized — not in Google Fonts import**
- index.html loads Inter at 300–700, Fraunces at 300–600. fontWeight 800/900 appears 15+ times across Home.jsx, JobCard, UpcomingCard, MissionIntel. Fraunces 700 at Home.jsx:780.
- Fix: Change all fontWeight 800/900 to 700 (Inter max loaded). Change Fraunces 700 to 600. Optionally add Inter 800 to the link tag.

**[P1] Nested button-in-button on the GO button**
- Home.jsx:858 is a button containing another button at line 899 (the refresh ↻). Invalid HTML. Browser may fire outer handler despite stopPropagation. Screen readers handle inconsistently.
- Fix: Convert outer to div with role="button" + tabIndex, or restructure refresh button as adjacent element.

**[P1] inkMuted (#8D6E63) fails WCAG AA contrast in light mode (~3.4:1)**
- Used for card notes (10.5px), drive sub-labels (9px), service tags (9px), MissionIntel prep text (11px). WCAG AA requires 4.5:1 for normal text.
- Fix: Darken LIGHT_PALETTE.inkMuted from #8D6E63 to #6B4C44 (~5.0:1 on #FFF0F3). One line in tokens.js:27.

**[P2] Hero section label has 2.1:1 contrast in light mode**
- "TODAY · Jun 8" at 10px uses T.pinkLabel (#E91E6A) on gradient top #FF94BC. 2.1:1 fails even for large text.
- Fix: Use T.ink (#4E342E) for this label in light mode, or replace the pink gradient with a flat near-white hero.

**[P2] "Next up" section header bypasses the design system**
- Home.jsx:743 inline div at 13px/800/Inter/0.8px-spacing. All other section labels use SectionLabel (Fraunces/10px/600). Inconsistency is subtle but visible.
- Fix: Use SectionLabel with color={DEEP_ROSE} prop.

**[P2] transition: height causes layout thrash on keyboard spacer**
- Home.jsx:1118: height transition on keyboard-aware spacer. Layout property animation forces reflow on every frame.
- Fix: Switch to max-height transition with overflow: hidden.
