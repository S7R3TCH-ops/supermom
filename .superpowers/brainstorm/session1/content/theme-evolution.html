The Secret Sauce for "Bold yet Airy":

  • The Core Pink: Swapping the "Electronic/Corporate" Pink (#E91E6A) for a "Fruit/Skin" Bold Pink (#FF70A6 or #F472B6). It's just as bright, but feels more organic.
  • The "Air": Using a Warm Cream (#FFF9F5) instead of a Cool Grey or Flat White background.
  • The "BAM": Using the bold pink for large elements like FABs, Hero buttons, and Icons, but surrounding them with 2x more whitespace than we have now.
  • Typography: Using the Serif (Fraunces) for almost everything meaningful to get that "Premium Magazine" look.
