Theme Exploration: Supermom Refresh

Drawing inspiration from The Birds Papaya and Sabrina Zohar

Mood & Color Directions

Both sites share a warm, editorial, and authentic vibe. They use soft fleshy tones, warm neutrals, and high-contrast typography.

A

The "Papaya" (Warm & Airy)

Soft pinks, terracotta, and cream backgrounds. Uses elegant serifs (like Fraunces) but with more breathing room. Feels like a premium lifestyle magazine.

B

The "Unfiltered" (Bold & Neutral)

High-contrast monochromatic base (off-black and ivory) with muted rose and sand accents. Bold uppercase headings. Feels expert, sisterly, and "No BS".

Proposed Changes

  • Softening the Pink: Moving away from the current "Hot Pink" toward "Dusty Rose" or "Soft Papaya".
  • Typography Shift: Leaning harder into the Fraunces serif for an editorial feel, or using bold uppercase sans-serifs for a more modern expert look.
  • Whitespace: Increasing margins and padding to create that "Airy" feel Sandra likes.
