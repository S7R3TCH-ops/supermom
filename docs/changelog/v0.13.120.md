## v0.13.120 - Oct 25, 2026 (iOS PWA: View Invoice stays in-app)

Branch `fix/ios-view-invoice-inapp-nav`. NOT pushed. UNVERIFIED on a real iPhone.

**Problem:** "View Invoice" in `JobDetailSheet` and "Review & Send Invoice/Receipt" in `PostJobSheet` used `window.open('/i/<id>', '_blank')`. In an installed iOS home-screen PWA that may open a separate webview with different login/storage and `navigator.standalone` (WebKit bugs 247270 / 250651, confirmed on iOS 16; not tested on iOS 26.6.1).

**Fix:**
- New `src/lib/openInvoice.js` (tests in `src/lib/openInvoice.test.js`): `openInvoice({ invoiceId, navigate, onClose, closeAfterOpen, isStandalone })`.
  - `isStandalonePwa()` true (iOS home-screen PWA only): `onClose()` then `navigate('/i/<id>', { replace: true })`. No `window.open`.
  - Otherwise (desktop, Android incl. installed PWAs, browser tabs): unchanged, `window.open('/i/<id>', '_blank')`. `JobDetailSheet` still does not close the sheet; `PostJobSheet` passes `closeAfterOpen: true` so it still closes right after opening.
- `replace: true` on purpose: the open sheet's `useBackClose` pushed a synthetic same-URL history entry. Replacing it means the invoice page's "Back" (`navigate(-1)`) lands straight on the page the sheet was opened from. A plain push would (by code trace, not device-tested) leave a dead extra Back press (e.g. ClientProfile -> sheet -> invoice -> Back -> ClientProfile's own `navigate(-1)` arrow would land on the same page). `useBackClose` itself is untouched; it sees the URL change and skips its own `history.back()`.
- `PostJobSheet.jsx` now calls `useNavigate()`; it only mounts via `PostJobSheetProvider` inside `<BrowserRouter>` (App.jsx), same as `JobDetailSheet`.
- Not changed: `InvoiceView.jsx`, `useBackClose.js`, maps `window.open` calls (Home, Calendar, CapeUpButton: external Google Maps URLs), `sms:` in NudgeDraftSheet.

**Behaviour difference in the installed iOS PWA:** returning from the invoice lands on the previous page with the sheet closed (the sheet providers live under the `*` route and unmount when `/i/:id` renders). In browser mode the original tab keeps the sheet open.

**UNVERIFIED on a real iPhone - Sandra test:**
1. Open the installed Supermom app (home-screen icon), open any job that has an invoice, tap "View Invoice". Expect the invoice page to appear inside the app, already logged in (no login screen).
2. Tap "<- Back" at the top of the invoice. Expect to land on the screen you started from, one tap, sheet closed.
3. Complete a job (PostJobSheet) and tap "Review & Send Invoice" / "Review & Send Receipt". Expect the same in-app invoice page and a one-tap Back.
4. On the invoice page tap "Download PDF". Expect the share sheet (v0.13.119 path, which may now be exercised for the first time from the main window).
5. Same flow in Android/desktop browser: expect a new tab, unchanged.
6. Open an invoiced job from a job opened over another sheet (e.g. Finance -> a payment detail -> the job), tap "View Invoice", then "Back". Expect the previous screen; if the first Back lands on the same screen with nothing open, that is the known nested-sheet dead entry (auditor A2, harmless, one extra Back).
